Plan a 3-day Jaipur trip on a student budget.

Here's a simple plan that keeps costs low:

Day 1: Amber Fort in the morning, then Panna Meena ka Kund and sunset at Nahargarh Fort.

Day 2: City Palace, Jantar Mantar and Hawa Mahal, then street food for dinner.

Day 3: Albert Hall Museum, then shopping at Johari and Bapu Bazaar.

Tip: a composite ticket covers several monuments, and students with ID pay less.

Give every visitor this voice

Your key only works in this browser. To make visitors hear the ElevenLabs voice too, generate the narration pack once, unzip it next to index.html (it creates an audio folder) and upload the site again. Visitors then play those files, with no key and no extra credits.

Scroll video

The film section draws its animation live. To scrub your own video while scrolling instead, preview it here. To keep it, put the file next to index.html and set scrollVideo at the top of the script, for example 'media/film.mp4'.
